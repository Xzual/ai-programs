## Native-Only Trust Boundary

- Advanced trusted-native kinds are rejected by the generic WebView ingest command with `ADVANCED_NATIVE_PUBLICATION_RUST_ONLY`.
- Advanced records are generated by the Rust adapters and passed to a crate-private publication function.
- HTTP success requires both status `202` and `success:true`.
- Network failure or any non-accepted backend response revokes the native producer session.
- An advanced publication failure also revokes advanced artifacts, downloads, scenes, Shadow Mode, and restore leases.
- The producer sequence advances only after accepted publication.
- `controlPlaneConnected` and advanced producer `available` remain false after bootstrap and become true only after at least one accepted ingest.

## Adapter Publication

Rust-side publication is connected for:

- real Windows power/battery and safe idle-presence metadata;
- owner-selected download telemetry with measured bytes and evidence-gated ETA;
- Visual Bookmark metadata and optional opaque screenshot handle;
- metadata-only workspace snapshots;
- reversible scene state and scene reversion;
- watcher records fixed to `configuration_required`;
- explicit, metadata-only Shadow Mode state;
- bounded Smart Retry plans with a maximum of two attempts and no action dispatch.

The watcher validator rejects `active` or `triggered` publication. It accepts only `event_based` plus `configuration_required`, with no `triggeredAt`, until a real approved OS event source exists.

## Lifecycle Revocation

- Owner logout/revocation clears the producer session and owner-bound advanced state.
- Owner rotation, stale lineage, expired producer sessions, replay, and authentication failure fail closed at the backend boundary and revoke local publication authority.
- Kill-switch detection revokes both advanced state and the native producer session before an advanced publication can continue.
- App shutdown and app exit clear the bridge, producer session, advanced leases, screenshot bytes, and restore child processes.
